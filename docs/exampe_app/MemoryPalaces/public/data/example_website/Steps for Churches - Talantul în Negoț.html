Steps for Churches

How your church can participate in Talantul în Negoț

1

Church Decision

Church pastors & leaders make the decision to participate in the contest. This is a church-wide commitment to encourage Biblical study.

2

Choose a Contest Leader

Church pastors choose a main contest leader for the church who will coordinate all activities and communication.

3

Contact the Organizers

Church leadership and church contest leader(s) contact the coordinators for more information:

  • Daniel Neagoe
  • Persida Bujdei
  • Daniela Barbura

4

Join the WhatsApp Group

Leaders will be added to the WhatsApp group for America where all announcements and coordination happens.

5

Organize Study Sessions

Organize and plan regular study sessions with groups. Use our online platform for practice questions and games.

6

Stay Updated

Regularly check the WhatsApp group for important information and announcements about contest dates, materials, and updates.
