Featured Game

Daily Quiz Challenge

A new quiz every day with competitive rankings and bonus rewards

How It Works

1

New Quiz Every Day

A fresh set of 10 questions is generated daily at midnight. Everyone gets the same questions.

2

One Attempt Per Day

You get ONE chance to complete the daily quiz. No restarts, no do-overs. Think carefully!

3

Timed Competition

Answer quickly! Your completion time is tracked and used for tiebreakers on the podium.

4

Daily Podium

Top 3 finishers earn podium positions with bonus points. Rankings update in real-time!

Rewards & Bonuses

1st Place - Gold

Bonus Points

Highest score + fastest time wins gold! Points vary by difficulty.

2nd Place - Silver

Bonus Points

Runner-up earns silver! Points scale with quiz difficulty.

3rd Place - Bronze

Bonus Points

Third place earns bronze! Rewards based on performance.

Streak Bonuses

Complete daily quizzes consecutively to build your streak. Longer streaks unlock higher multipliers and bonus rewards!

Note: Exact point values vary based on quiz difficulty, question count, and other factors.
