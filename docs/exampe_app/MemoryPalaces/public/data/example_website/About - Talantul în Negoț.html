About the Contest

Talantul în Negoț

A Bible Contest celebrating spiritual talents

Our History

Talantul în Negoț is a Bible Contest that began in Romania over 25 years ago. It has expanded to 19 countries, 452 churches, and nearly 10,000 annual contestants.

In 2024, America (US & Canada) joined the contest with 12 churches and in 2025 with 26 churches. Our mission is to inspire individuals to deepen their understanding of the Scriptures and grow in their faith in the Lord Jesus Christ.

"I have hidden your word in my heart, that I may not sin against you" — Psalms 119:11

Two Branches of Competition

Contestants may participate in one or both branches

Bible Knowledge

A test of Biblical knowledge based on age groups and bibliography. Contestants study throughout the year and take a 1-hour written test on the day of the contest.

  • Age-appropriate study materials
  • Written examination format
  • Practice with our online platform
Practice Now

Memorization

ALL age groups memorize the same Book of the Bible. In 2026: John chapters 14, 15, 16 (NIV). Contestants recite in front of judges.

  • Same material for all ages
  • Oral recitation to judges
  • Flashcard practice available
